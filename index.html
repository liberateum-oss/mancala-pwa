How to Play Mancala

Setup: Each of the 6 pits on your side starts with 4 stones. The large stores on each end start empty.
Your Turn: Click one of your pits to pick up all the stones there. They get distributed one-by-one into each pit counter-clockwise, including your store, but skipping the opponent's store.
Bonus Turn: If your last stone lands in your store, you get another turn.
Capture: If your last stone lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture it AND all stones in the directly opposite opponent's pit — both go into your store.
Game Over: When all pits on one side are empty, the player with stones remaining collects them all into their store. Most stones in the store wins.
Opponent Moves: This app is a two-player tracker. When your opponent makes a move in the physical game, click their pit directly on the board to keep the app in sync and get suggestions.
Pit Numbering: Your pits are numbered 1–6 left to right. Opponent pit #1 sits directly opposite your pit #1, so corresponding pits share a number across the board.
